Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 7 September 2026

1. Refund window

Request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if Pro does not meet your needs. Email support@fenwicklabs.com with your billing email and PayPal / Razorpay transaction ID.

2. How refunds are issued

Refunds are returned through the original payment method (PayPal or Razorpay). Processing time depends on the gateway and your bank.

Monthly and yearly Pro ends on the expiry date shown in your confirmation email unless you renew. Lifetime is one-time and non-recurring. After expiry, the account returns to Free limits.

4. Charge disputes

Contact us first so we can resolve issues quickly. Gateway dispute processes remain available under PayPal and Razorpay rules.
