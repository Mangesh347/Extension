Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026 · Fenwick Labs

1. What we collect

  • Account email when you sign in or pay (used for Pro entitlement).
  • Payment confirmation metadata from PayPal / Razorpay (never full card numbers).
  • Anonymous device install IDs to enforce the 2-profile Pro limit.
  • Local chat/folder/notes data stored in your browser via Chrome storage.

2. Payments

Payments are processed by PayPal and Razorpay. Review their privacy policies. We store hashed email and entitlement status on our secure backend (Supabase) to unlock Pro.

3. How we use data

To deliver the extension, verify Pro, send renewal notices, and provide support. We do not sell personal data.

4. Retention

Entitlement records are kept while your license is active and as required for accounting. You may request deletion of account-linked records by emailing support (subject to legal retention).
