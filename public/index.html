FAQ

Clear answers before you upgrade.

Is this an official Anthropic product?

No. Claude Enhancer is an unofficial Chrome extension by Fenwick Labs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic, PBC. Claude is a trademark of Anthropic.

Why does checkout show a higher total than $4 / $40 / $80?

Base prices stay $4 / $40 / $80. Checkout adds applicable tax (18% GST) so the amount due is $4.72 / $47.20 / $94.40 — always shown before you pay.

Which payment methods are supported?

PayPal and Razorpay (cards / UPI). Card details stay with the payment provider — we never store them.

What happens when my plan ends?

Monthly and yearly Pro end on the expiry date. We email a reminder beforehand. Lifetime does not expire. After expiry you return to Free limits.

Can I use one Pro email on many browsers?

Each paid email supports up to two Chrome profiles.

Do I need a license key?

No. After payment, open Claude with the same billing email — Pro unlocks automatically.
