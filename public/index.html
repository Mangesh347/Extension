FAQ

Clear answers before you pay.

Why do home and checkout show different totals?

They do not for base price: Monthly $4, Yearly $40, Lifetime $80. Checkout adds 18% GST so the total due is higher ($4.72 / $47.20 / $94.40). That is intentional and shown on the payment page.

Which payment methods are supported?

PayPal and Razorpay only. Razorpay hosts the card form; we never store card numbers.

What happens when my month ends?

Pro deactivates on the expiry date. We email a renewal reminder beforehand. Lifetime does not expire.

Can I share one Pro email across many browsers?

No. Each paid email is limited to two Chrome profiles to protect paying customers.
